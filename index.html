 +
Procedural Generation UI / UX Design Multiplayer 3D modeling Lighting Post Process Unity / C#

03

Shift

Solo Project · gravity switching first person shooter with abilities

 +
Unreal / Blueprints Lighting Level Design

Gravity switching first person movement shooter designed to be a casual arcade like experience. Players will jump and step on planes allowing them to switch gravity. With a variety of gamemodes this experience is designed to be a 3vs3 for constant action and strategy. Levels were designed in a way that allows players to be creative in their approach when facing enemy elements.

Designed a gravity orientation mechanic that uses volume triggers to change gravity surface. Designed a functional weapon and ablity system chosen at spawn. Developed and lit multiple levels in a way that allows any section of the map to look as if it were in a movie regardless of where the player was standing.

About

I got into the inital phase of game design from a very young age, always interested in how the game ive come to know and love are built. I started off by watching tutorials and experimenting on my own via various projects. Most notable was Shift. Designing games that were out of scope taught me many lessons and the pillars of game design. Through my failures i've learned a great many things and actively apply them in which ever project im currently working on. I'm currently attending the University of Central Florida. At UCF the classes have helped give me a clearer perspective of what the industry may hold for me and hardships yet to come. Throughout my time at UCF the Tech Art pathway stood out to me as it acts like a bridge between modeling and programming. This role allows me actively do more for my team and really gave me a path that I can be passionate about. Using my new found experience and knowledge I expect to graduate in Spring 2027 with a bachelors degree in Digital Media.

Most recently i've been working on several projects for multiple classes including Workshop and Casual Game Production as well as my own passion project called Shift. I actively spend many hours modeling and programming these various projects. I always ensure to check in with leads before making changes and have a strict focus on creating, modular, optomized, clean, well commented code.
